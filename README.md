# StatLink

**StatLink** is an interactive academic family tree and dissertation landscape for **Statistics, Biostatistics, Probability, and related quantitative sciences**, unifying **550,000+ PhD scholars** and **590,000+ doctoral mentorship lineages** across **3,000+ universities worldwide**.

🌐 **Live Site**: [https://yuzhao-ucsd.github.io/StatLink/](https://yuzhao-ucsd.github.io/StatLink/)

## Main Features

- **Fuzzy Search & Lineage Filters**: Google-style search with instant typo-tolerant autocomplete, nickname & compound-name matching, and filters by **Disciplinary Scope**, **University** (PhD Alumni and/or Faculty), **PhD Graduation Year**, and **Descendant Of** (browse all academic descendants or direct PhD students of any scholar).
- **Interactive Family Tree Visualizer**: Explore multi-generation doctoral ancestor and descendant DAGs in vertical or horizontal layouts with pan/zoom camera controls, editable generation depth (`Ancestors ↑` / `Descendants ↓`), and **Light / Dark** themes.
- **Scholar Dossiers & World-Famous Scientist Ancestors**: Inspect dissertation titles, scholar portraits, verified external links (Personal Website, Wikipedia, Google Scholar, LinkedIn, ORCID, Wikidata), direct students, academic siblings, and step-by-step lineage paths tracing back to **109 historical and modern scientific pioneers** (e.g., Gauss, Laplace, Euler, Fisher, Neyman, Blackwell).
- **Academic Kinship (Common Ancestor Finder)**: Trace the shortest shared doctoral lineage and Most Recent Common Academic Ancestor (MRCA) connecting any two researchers.
- **Field Analytics & Dissertation Topic Landscape**: Explore annual PhD graduation trends (`1920–2026`), top PhD-granting institutions, and an interactive **2D Dissertation Topic Landscape** built with language-invariant, statistics-fine-tuned embeddings clustering **65,000+ dissertations** across major statistical research domains.
